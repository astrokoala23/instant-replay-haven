# Instant Replay

Instant Replay is a game created for Hackclub Haven themed on the topic of **Bounce Back**. We used this theme to create a top-down museum theft game where you can use multiple copies of yourself to solve puzzles in the game.

## How to Play

Watch the Intro Video Here: [https://user-cdn.hackclub-assets.com/01a1df56-960d-702b-affd-f2878e3cf967/download.mp4](https://user-cdn.hackclub-assets.com/01a1df56-960d-702b-affd-f2878e3cf967/download.mp4)

Our game is listed on itch.io where you can view it [here]. The player can be controlled by WASD or the arrow keys, everytime you touch a pressure plate you are taken back to your starting position and a duplicatre version of yourself repeats the action you did. You can use this to hold down multiple pressure plates at the same time to unlock doors.

## Backstory

Our game is centered around a main character, the thief, who sees a news broadcast of the *Lona Misa* being shipped to the *Ouvrel museum*. This happens to be close to where he lives so he decides to steal it. He uses his temporal discharge gun to create multiple copies of himself from his past which is how we navigates the different levels.
